# 2026-06-24 - Tester Feedback Portal And Triage Flow

- Status: approved
- Decision owner: Bryant
- Decision: External tester feedback should flow through the professional testing company's portal using a structured Crate intake form. Bryant reviews first for product judgment and privacy, Codex synthesizes reports into triage classifications, engineering bugs route to the Autonomous Crate Failure Loop, and UX/design feedback routes to Jenna's redesign backlog.
- Context: Bryant plans to hire testers through a professional testing site before public release. Crate feedback needs to be structured enough to distinguish product feedback, setup issues, privacy-sensitive artifacts, expected limitations, and true app bugs.
- Rationale: A single portal-based intake path prevents feedback from scattering across email, texts, screenshots, ChatGPT messages, and ad hoc notes. Structured questions make bug triage faster and preserve privacy boundaries before Codex or engineering loops inspect artifacts.
- Implications: Tester onboarding and portal forms should include Crate version, macOS version, workflow tested, package result, expected included/excluded assets, package counts, Package Details observations, safe artifacts, reproducibility, warning text, Figma-specific context, privacy status, and severity from the tester's perspective.
- Non-goals: This decision does not choose the testing vendor, create the portal, upload artifacts, inspect private tester files, change app code, change crate-web, or mutate release state.
- Applies to: closed beta tester intake, portal form design, QA synthesis, bug triage, Jenna redesign backlog, support routing, and future tester handoffs.
- Does not apply to: Jenna internal QA prompts, local manual QA where Bryant directly supervises, or emergency engineering triage with already-approved artifacts.
